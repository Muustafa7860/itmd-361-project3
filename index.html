My Future Career: Web Developer

Welcome! My name is Mustafa Aziz, and I am a student at Illinois Institute of Technology in Chicago.

Why Web Development?

I enjoy turning an idea into something people can open in a browser and use. Web development combines logic, creativity, and problem solving, and every project teaches me something new.

What a Web Developer Does

  • Builds the structure of web pages with HTML
  • Styles pages so they look great on any screen with CSS
  • Adds interactivity with JavaScript
  • Makes sites accessible to everyone
  • Tests and fixes problems before sites go live

My Career Goals

  1. Finish my degree and build a strong portfolio of projects.
  2. Complete a summer internship at a Chicago technology company.
  3. Start my career as a front-end developer.
  4. Grow into a full-stack developer who leads projects.

Skills I Am Learning

Right now I am learning semantic HTML, how to validate my code with the W3C validator, and how to organize files in a clean directory structure. Soon I will add CSS, JavaScript, and version control with Git.

How I Got Started

My path began in the classroom, teaching and tutoring students. Explaining ideas clearly is now the skill I want to bring to building websites.
